These are a few of my Favorite Things!

Welcome to my website! Please, come in, take a look around, and try not to trip over anything on your way in. This is a little corner of the internet dedicated to some of the most treasured residents of my room. They may not pay rent, contribute to the groceries, or help with the occasional cleanup, but they have certainly earned their place here.

Now, when I say these are some of the things in my room that are more frequently loved than the others, I don't necessarily mean I spend hours admiring them every day (although I can't deny that happens). What I really mean is that they receive the princess treatment: dusting, a little rearranging, and perhaps an unnecessary amount of attention as compared to everything else in my room. Meanwhile, the objects sitting quietly in the background are probably wondering what they did wrong (sorry guys....)

Think of this website as a tiny museum of my personal interests, except there are no admission fees, no security guards telling you not to touch anything, and absolutely no guarantee that the curator is mentally prepared to explain why a particular item is so special. Here, you'll find my favorite things, the things that bring me joy, and a few honorable mentions that narrowly escaped being left out of the spotlight.

So go ahead and explore! Browse through the collections, admire the questionable distribution of my free time, and discover what has successfully secured a permanent spot in my room. Just remember: every item here has a story, every story has a reason, and every reason probably involves me saying, "Okay, but look how cool it is!"

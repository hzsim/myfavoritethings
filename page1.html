Gojo Shibuya Incident Figurine

The story

As of right now, one of my biggest interest is Jujutsu Kaisen (JJK) by Gege Akutami. While this interest should probably cost me no more than a netflix subscription, my wallet begs to differ.
This is one of the many JJK figurines that I own, but I particularly love this the most because I won it from the claw machine at Round1 which took a number of tries (don't ask how many)... This is from one of the arcs in the anime that I really enjoyed watching because it was such a tense moment in the series as previous events have been leading up to this incident. Overall I just thought it was so cool and finding out that this figurine existed made me extremely happy :'D

My thoughts

Quality: 10/10

Favorite feature: The details in the debris is so cool! I really like how the sign has been damaged by the incident. I also really love the detail put into the features of the character. I think this is one of the few figurines that display character features extremely well.

Brand: SEGA
